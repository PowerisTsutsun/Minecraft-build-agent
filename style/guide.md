# House style

Taste only. Everything geometric is enforced in code now - roof pitch, eaves,
stair treads, window frames, quoins, what floats and what does not - so rules
about those belong in the primitives, not here.

Read fresh on every !make, so saving this file changes the next build. 8000
character cap. Delete anything you disagree with.

## Palette

- Default to gothic or medieval_stone for anything defensive, nordic for
  anything domestic and wooden, desert only when the request asks for warmth.
- Never the same block for walls and roof.
- Weathering around 0.12. Higher on ruins, lower on anything meant to look new.

## Proportions

- A tower reads best at 4 to 6 times its own width in height.
- A keep should be taller than its towers; towers should differ from each other.
- Storeys of 5. Grand halls 6 or more.
- Odd footprints, so doors and ridges land on a centre block.

## Always

- Two or three masses of different heights, one clearly dominant. A single box
  is never the answer, even for a cottage - add a porch, a chimney, a wing.
- A door on the side the player is standing on.
- Something bespoke that the decorator cannot know about: a well, a portcullis,
  a market stall, a garden. That is what makes a place look inhabited.

## Never

- A flat roof, or a roof shorter than half the wall height.
- A wall longer than 10 blocks with nothing on it.
- Hand-placed ornament that a decorator habit already covers.
- Decoration on a machine. Set decor to plain for anything functional.
